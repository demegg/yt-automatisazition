Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

ShortForge runs on your machine or server. Video files and generated clips are stored locally unless you choose to upload them to YouTube or TikTok.

Data we handle

  • Videos you upload and shorts you generate (stored on your device/server)
  • Optional account email if you enable sign-in
  • OAuth tokens for YouTube/TikTok when you connect those platforms (stored locally in the app database)

Third parties

When you connect YouTube or TikTok, those platforms receive data according to their own privacy policies. We do not sell your personal data.
